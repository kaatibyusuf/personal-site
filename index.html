~/papers

Papers I have written and published. Each one has a short note on what it argues.

  • On the Shared Mathematics of Dynamical Systems and Neural Computation Neural networks are often built by trial and error, yet the mathematics behind them is old. This paper traces calculus, variational principles, statistical mechanics and symmetry from physics into machine learning, shows where the two are the same mathematics under a change of variables, and says plainly where the match is only an analogy. It argues that neural computation should be designed by modelling and calculation, not by guesswork. Mathematical physics · Machine learning · 20 pages
  • Solids of Revolution: Applications in Manufacturing I took four everyday products (a PET bottle, an aluminium pot, a light-bulb envelope and a CNC-turned flashlight body), modelled their profiles, and used the disk, washer, shell and surface-area integrals in Python to compute volume, surface area, mass and toolpath length. Predicted volumes came within 2.52% of water-displacement measurements. The point is that undergraduate calculus is enough to do real manufacturing work. Applied mathematics · Manufacturing · 11 pages
  • The Lost Compass: Reclaiming Islamic Values in an Age of Moral Relativism A written submission to the Da'wah Webinar of the Nigerian Association of Muslim Law Students at Olabisi Onabanjo University. It describes how a generation drifts from fixed moral direction to convenience, answers legal positivism and relativism from the Islamic tradition, warns against hunting for concessions, and closes with four ways to reclaim the compass. Islamic ethics · Law · 14 pages
