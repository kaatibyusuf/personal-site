~/about

I'm Abdulsamad, a software engineering student at Miva Open University. I build the products I wish existed for my own community, mostly in education, learning and faith.

I run One Growth, where I teach writing and coach people through habits that last past the first week. I'm also the founder of Himaayah Schools, which is the first piece of a longer plan to build in education, healthcare, technology and philanthropy.

I don't take live speaking engagements at the moment. If you'd like me to contribute to something, write to me and I'll reply in writing.
